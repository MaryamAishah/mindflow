How It Works

  1. Create an account on MindFlow
  2. Click the 'Synthesize a Mind Map' button
  3. Select the type of input (text, upload, web)
  4. Paste/upload your content, or, if you want to generate a mind map for a general topic, select 'web' and enter the topic
  5. Select the theme, colours, and fonts for your mind map
  6. Wait for the magic to happen!
  7. Once your mind map is ready, you can interact with it, collapse/expand its sections, and edit it as you desire. You can also download it as a JPG or PDF file.
